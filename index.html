Engineered to Eliminate Dead Space Tissuelock solves for head‑to‑toe surgical closure

Seroma, hematoma, and infection are common complications following large soft tissue surgery, occurring in 20-30% of cases. Incisional and excisional wound failure occurs in 8-13% of procedures, leading to prolonged recovery. Dead space is the underlying determinant, nine times out of ten.

Tissuelock is a first-in-class product designed to close internal tissue planes and improve surgical outcomes.

Closes Dead Space

Bonds tissue layers together and prevents fluid accumulation

No Drains or Tissue Damage

Eliminates need for drains and no specialized sutures

Promotes Natural Healing

Supports tissue integration and accelerates regeneration

The Current Standard of Care Requires Change

Drains are a workaround, not a fix.

Following large soft tissue repair and reconstructive procedures, patients go home with painful drains and a log book. Surgeons accept the burden because the alternative, fluid collecting, in an open plane is worse. Yet, it still happens. Patients recover worse, and surgeons spend more time back in the OR.

We discovered the upstream solution: eliminate the dead space.
